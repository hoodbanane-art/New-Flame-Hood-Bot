## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-13

### Added

- Discord slash commands for kicking, banning, unbanning, listing bans, and restarting servers.
- Roblox Open Cloud integration and a server-side MessagingService listener.
- Role, user, administrator, guild, and protected-player authorization checks.
- Confirmation prompts and Discord audit logging.
- Authenticated, versioned kick messages with replay and expiry protection.
- Automated tests and production dependency auditing.
