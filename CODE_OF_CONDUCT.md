# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing credentials, moderation records, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language and imagery.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Abusing this project to target Roblox users, Discord communities, or systems without authorization.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [Focalorrr GitHub profile](https://github.com/focalorrr). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
